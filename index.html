01 /The idea

Most takes die on the second line. You lose your place, glance away, start over. TapMade keeps the words beside the lens and moves them only when you speak.

02 /Four ways to film

One camera screen for everything

  • Script

    Front camera in 4K or 1080p with the teleprompter. Scroll by hand, set a speed, or follow your voice.

  • Dual

    Front and back cameras at once — stacked, picture-in-picture or full screen.

  • React

    Pick a video from your library and film yourself under it.

  • Podcast

    One wide camera, two wireless mics. Auto Edit cuts to whoever is talking, in 9:16.

03 /Captions

Captions that spell your words right

Speech is matched against your own script, so names and slang come out the way you wrote them. Karaoke, Pop Word, Slide Up, Minimal and more — exported at 1080×1920.
