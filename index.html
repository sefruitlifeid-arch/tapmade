01 /The idea

Most takes die on the second line. You lose your place, glance away, start over. TapMade keeps the words beside the lens and moves them only when you speak.

02 /Teleprompter

It scrolls when you talk

Follow voice listens on your iPhone and moves the script with you. Pause to think and it waits. Skip a line and it catches up. English and Indonesian.

  • Follow voiceScrolls with your speech, on device
  • SpeedA steady pace you set
  • By handDrag it yourself
03 /Captions

Stop recording. Captions are done.

Every word is matched against your own script, so names and slang come out the way you wrote them. Pick a style, export 1080×1920, post.

04 /Thumbnail

The thumbnail comes from the take

TapMade picks your best frame and writes the prompt. Choose a look — bold, editorial, tech — and finish it in your own ChatGPT app.

Mockups. The image is made in ChatGPT from your frame and the prompt TapMade prepares.

05 /Dual camera

Both cameras, your layout

Film what you see and your face at the same time. Switch layouts while you record and style the bubble the way you like it.

06 /Podcast

Two mics in. Auto-framed out.

Set one wide camera on the table and clip on two wireless mics. Auto Edit finds each face, frames it in 9:16 and cuts to whoever is talking.
