Privacy Policy

TapMade collects nothing. Last updated 31 October 2026.

We don't collect your data

TapMade has no account and no sign-in. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking. We do not collect, store or receive your videos, audio, scripts or any personal information. Your projects stay in the app's storage on your iPhone.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera — to record your video.
  • Microphone — to record your voice, including through wireless mics.
  • Speech recognition — so the teleprompter can follow your voice and captions can be timed. Recognition runs on your iPhone; audio never leaves the device.
  • Add to Photos — to save videos you choose to export. TapMade cannot read your other photos or videos. Videos you pick from your library are accessed only through the system picker.

Sharing

When you choose to share a video, frame or prompt (for example to another app such as ChatGPT), it is sent through the iOS share sheet to the app you pick. What that app does with it is governed by its own privacy policy. TapMade sends nothing on its own.

Children

TapMade does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete a project inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
