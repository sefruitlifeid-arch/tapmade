Get in touch

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email dearhuman.workspace@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

My wireless mic isn't used

Plug the receiver in before opening the recording screen. TapMade shows the active mic at the top of the camera; if it says iPhone mic, unplug and reconnect the receiver.

The teleprompter doesn't follow my voice

Check that Speech Recognition and Microphone are allowed in Settings → TapMade, and that the script language (English or Indonesian) matches how you speak.

Where are my videos?

Inside TapMade until you tap Save to Photos. Deleting the app deletes its projects, so save the ones you want to keep first.

The phone gets warm with Dual Camera

Running two cameras is demanding. Use 1080p, keep the phone out of direct sun, and take short breaks between long takes.
